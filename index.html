01 / The journey

From service to a life of study.

A career spanning the armed forces, corporate leadership and the written word.

A Botany Honours graduate, Capt. Alok Jain served the Indian Army as a Short Service Commissioned Officer in the Corps of Air Defence from 1991 to 1998.

He moved into civilian leadership in 1999, spending the next 19 years with organisations including Ashiana Housing and Finance India Ltd, Oswal Mills, Jones Lang LaSalle, Discovery Channel and Vodafone. His work covered infrastructure, facilities, procurement, health and safety.

While serving as GM, Property and Facilities Head at Vodafone Delhi NCR, he spent nearly six years writing the manuscript that became The Root Cause. In 2017, he left his corporate career to dedicate himself to publishing and exploring Ayurveda, astrology, yoga and Vedic philosophy.

02 / Published work

The Root Cause

Ayurveda, Astrology and Beyond for a Blissful Life

The series is the outcome of years spent examining ancient knowledge through contemporary language. Jain discusses Ayurvedic principles, the traditional doshas of Vata, Pitta and Kapha, the six tastes, yoga, astrology and the symbolism of Vedic narratives.

Across revised editions and subsequent volumes, he draws on subjects such as biology, astronomy, geography, physics and chemistry to develop his own interpretations of the relationship between the human body, behaviour and the wider universe.

03 / Fields of exploration

Three connected interests.

His writings interpret traditional concepts through an interdisciplinary lens, bringing together spirituality, lifestyle, ancient texts and ideas from modern disciplines.

I

Ayurveda & everyday life

The doshas, food, the six tastes, herbs, daily habits and the role of lifestyle in traditional systems of well-being.

II

Yoga, astrology & philosophy

Ashtanga Yoga, pranayama, mantras and the author's interpretations of planets, zodiac signs and individual experience.

III

Mythology & inner meaning

The ideas behind Devas, Asuras and other mythological narratives, considered through ethics, behaviour and personal growth.

04 / His perspective

Knowledge, action and inner transformation.

For Jain, Vedic teachings offer a framework for understanding the mind, body, conduct and spiritual life. His books encourage readers to examine how personal choices may shape their present and future.

01

Knowledge before belief

He encourages readers to understand traditional concepts rather than follow them mechanically, drawing connections with the language of modern disciplines.

02

Actions can change the future

In his astrological worldview, future outcomes are not fixed: knowledge (Gyan Yoga), action (Karma and Kriya Yoga) and faith (Bhakti Yoga) can shape choices.

03

Food, lifestyle and awareness

He places Ayurveda and everyday choices at the centre of his interpretation of physical, mental and spiritual well-being.

04

Ethics and the meaning of mythology

He reads the Devas and Asuras as symbols of qualities within people and describes a journey from Kalyuga's conflict toward Satyuga's harmony.

These are the author's philosophical and interpretive perspectives. Claims connecting astrology to quantum physics or particular medical outcomes are not established scientific conclusions.
